## 2026-06-23 - Reusing combined vector search results for graph seeds
**Learning:** In multi-step retrieval pipelines (like TEMPR), subsequent stages often require entry points/seeds that were already computed by earlier stages. In Hindsight's `retrieve_all_fact_types_parallel`, the graph retrieval was originally repeating the HNSW vector search to find semantic seeds, ignoring the fact that `retrieve_semantic_bm25_combined` and `retrieve_temporal_combined` had already fetched the most relevant semantic/temporal memories. Re-using these results directly bypasses redundant database queries entirely.
**Action:** Always look for opportunities to pass data laterally between parallel or sequential retrieval pipeline stages, specifically bypassing duplicate embedding comparisons or DB queries. Ensure thresholds are applied correctly when reusing data meant for a different purpose.
